Evaluating Integrity, Creativity,Skill Level and the Role of "Vibe Coders"

By Sophia Renaud R. Sacriz

Imagine a documentation team offering a full wedding package using just an iPhone 16 Pro Max for filming and CapCut for same-day edits. They claim two years of experience and an above-average portfolio. Would you accept it?

Personally I will agree because the price range offered equates to their equipment. Another point is that through their experiences and portfolio they have proved themselves that they have good integrity. It showed that it created quality outputs with only the use of a phone, simple editing software because of how well the portfolio that it is higher than average.

In the digital age, technology progresses, it innovates and generates. And through this progression, it aids human necessities making life easier. AI or Artificial Intelligence, is an epitome of advanced technologies that really helped humans. AI gives a great deal of help to the people, but it seems it is giving too much help to them.

In the field of technologies, there are different kinds of coders, and one of them are the vibe coders. By the term itself, vibe coders are those who go with the flow when coding. They don’t do hard code but rather direct AI tools to generate the code for them. If they don’t really code, do they have integrity? Are they really creative? Should they really be considered as programmers?

Correlating it with the wedding documentation dilemma, vibe coders are like the documentation team, even when using less effort in developing a program they can still create a good output. However, vibe coders must also leverage that they are still responsible for whatever the output will be. AI generated can also be more likely to have faulty codes and unclear documentation which lessen the integrity of the work.

Moreover their creativity shows well when they think on what to prompt, this shows that they have the vision on what to create. With the use of AI, they realized what can be the outcome, but AI has no brain, they don't really understand your prompt, it just executes it.

The skill level can be identified through experience and testimonials. Same with the documentation team, even with minimal equipment they showed what they are doing just because of their portfolio. Experience and output are crucial because it provides solid support on what they can really do.

Just like the documentation team, vibe coders can still be reliable, as long as they know what they are doing and they fully understand what they are doing.

AI is a very useful tool, but we people should not be too dependent on it. We are the who have brains, not the machine.
