Academics

When using Generative AI in academic settings, it is important to uphold academic integrity and originality, ensure transparency and critically evaluate the model's outputs to avoid misinformation. Gen AI is only used for support and enhancement, not as a substitute for original work. (Generative AI in Higher Education: A Global Perspective of Institutional Adoption Policies and Guidelines, n.d.)

Plagiarism or Misuse

To avoid plagiarism or misuse of Generative AI, it requires transparency about the tools you used, proper citation, and maintaining ethical standards. (LibGuides: Generative AI: ChatGPT: Citing Generative AI, n.d.)

Accuracy and Verification

Ensuring accuracy in Gen Ai is crucial in order to maintain reliabilty to the information. It requires humans to evaluate all outputs, cross-reference claims, and verify facts using credible sources before accepting or publicizing generated content. (Research Guides: Generative AI: Evaluating Output, n.d.)

Bias and Fairness

To ethically use Gen AI must apply fairness and transparency. One must be aware of he or she is using, promote fair use and maintain human judgement. Always remember to treat Gen AI as a responsible assistant, not a decision-maker. (What is AI ethics|IBM n.d.)

Safety and Reliability

Avoid inputting personal, confidential, or sensitive information into Generative AI tools for it may be used to train future models that can compromise your privacy and security. (Queen’s University Belfast, 2025)
