Frequently Asked Questions

Generative Artificial intelligence (Gen AI) is a subset of AI in which is designed to create new content such as text, images, et cetera based on existing data. Compared to traditional data, Gen AI focuses more on creating rather than analysis and interpretation.

Gen AI uses neural networks primarily to identify patterns in massive datasets of human-created content and then generates new content that is based on a vast and diverse range of data.

Some examples of Gen AI tools are ChatGPT for text generation, Midjourney for image generation, and Github Copilot for code generation.

Gen AI has the ability to generate diverse and original content that open humans to see different ideas or perspectives. This model can also boost organizational productivity, as it can reduce effort on heavy tasks, provide summaries and produce transcriptions and et cetera.

Though gen AI can be very useful, this also comes with a cost. Gen AI lacks authenticity because they don’t really understand the content but can only generate which can cause inaccuracies and biases. Gen AI can generate new content by imitating or summarising existing content, mostly without consent of the owners of the original content which creates issues in copyright and ownership. Moreover, Gen Ai indirectly creates large amounts of carbon that affects environments due to its huge amount of power consumption. And lastly, because of this existing model, there have been issues of exploitation in this field of work, causing a negative impact to workers involved.

Gen AI are used various fields, and those that are popular are content creations, art and design, automation, code generation, and healthcare.
